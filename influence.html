influence

Psychedelic & Classic Rock

  • Jimi Hendrix: Smith’s brother introduced him to Hendrix’s Purple Haze at age 8, sparking his fascination with psychedelic rock.
  • Thin Lizzy: He attended their concerts multiple times, admiring their powerful live sound.
  • David Bowie: Bowie’s Ziggy Stardust was the first album Smith ever bought, inspiring his theatrical persona and outsider identity.

Punk & Post-Punk

  • Siouxsie and the Banshees: Smith admired their “wall of noise” and even played guitar for them briefly.
  • The Buzzcocks: Their melodic punk style influenced Smith’s ambition to merge melody with noise, forming the foundation of The Cure’s sound
